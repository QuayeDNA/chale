Privacy Policy

Effective: — (draft) · Last updated: — (draft)

1. What we collect

  • Account basics — your email address and a display name, when you create an account. Browsing stays anonymous: guests get no email on file.
  • Your answers — text answers, voice recordings, and the questions they belong to.
  • Pairing data — who is paired with whom, and your invite code.
  • Favourites — the questions you heart.
  • Basic diagnostics — app version and crash data from the stores, when you've opted into sharing them.

2. How your answers are used

Answers exist to be revealed to your paired partner — that's the whole product. We don't read your answers, and we don't use them to train anything. Your partner sees your answer when the reveal happens; nobody else does.

3. Voice notes

Voice answers are recorded on your device, uploaded when you send, and deleted automatically after both of you have listened — or 7 days after sending, whichever comes first. If you choose to keep a voice note permanently, it stays until you delete it.

4. Who else touches your data

Chale runs on Supabase (hosting, database, and file storage) — a processor that stores data on our instruction. We don't sell your data and we don't share it with advertisers.

5. How long we keep things

  • Voice notes: until listened-to by both, or 7 days — unless kept.
  • Answers: while your account and connection exist.
  • Account data: until you ask us to delete it.

6. Your choices

You can delete your account and data from the app (Profile → Sign out, then contact us) or by emailing us. Deleting removes your profile, answers, and favourites; it can't un-reveal answers your partner has already seen.

7. Children

Chale is for users aged 13 and over. If you are under 16, a parent or guardian must review and agree to this policy and the Terms on your behalf, and is responsible for your use of the app. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child is using the app without that consent, contact us and we'll remove the account.

8. Changes

We'll post any new version of this policy here and update the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.
