Anatomy of one question
13 Either What moment made you feel truly understood by me?
ordinal is the authored position shown above · kind decides whether the answer panel offers typing, recording, or both · pack_id is the only thing tying it to a deck. Questions are later referenced by prompts.question_id and favourites.question_id, which is why seeds soft-retire rather than delete them.
